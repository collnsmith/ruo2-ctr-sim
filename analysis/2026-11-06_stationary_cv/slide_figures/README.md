# Slide figures

Made by `../make_slide_figures.py` from `../plot_data/`. Slides 4 and 11 also evaluate the CTR model. Each figure is named by
its slide in `../slides_outline.txt`.

- **Format:** 16:9, 150 dpi.
- **Colours:** one colour per film: 3.3 nm blue, 4.1 nm orange, 7 nm green.
- **Markers:** triangles are OH-blind control reflections.
- **Legends:** outside the data.
