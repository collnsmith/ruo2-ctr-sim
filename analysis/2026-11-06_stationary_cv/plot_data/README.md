# Plot data: stationary CVs and CTRs of the 3.3, 4.1 and 7 nm films

Everything here is generated by `../make_plot_data.py` from the raw ROI sums (`../data/`), the CTR project
(`../../2026-11-05_oh_fits/oh_rods_fit.ctrproj.json`) and the saved results in `..`. Rerun it instead of editing files.

Conventions:
- Plain CSV with one header row. Lines starting with `#` describe the file; skip them when reading
  (pandas: `pd.read_csv(f, comment="#")`).
- Changes are fractions (0.1 = 10%).
- Potentials are vs Ag/AgCl. `E_V_RHE` = E + 0.238 V (logbook).
- File names: `s<scan>_<H>_<K>_<L>`.

| Folder | Contents | Used for |
|---|---|---|
| `01_cv_traces/<film>/` | Every stationary scan, image by image: branch (before / hold / anodic / cathodic), assigned E, summed counts and their error, sum relative to the 0 V hold, Ru and Ti ROIs where separated | raw-trace plots (`fig_raw`, `look_*`) |
| `02_cv_curves/<film>/` | Cycle-averaged change vs E per sweep (0.05 V bins) for the sum, Ru and Ti; not made for 127 (25 mV/s) | `fig_cv` |
| `03_cv_summary/cv_amplitudes.csv` | One row per scan: film, reflection, OH-sensitive or control, timing source, best-sine peak-to-peak (sum, Ru, Ti), point noise, linear change 0 to 0.7 V, Ru/Ti counts | `fig_robust` panel a |
| `04_staircases/` | 224 and 225: traces and per-level means (not used in the argument) | backup B7 |
| `05_ctr_rods/<film>/` | CTR data per rod and condition: L, F, sigma, sigma_eff. 7 nm (0 1) at -0.1 and 0.7 V split into the two measurements: `_a` = 333/334 and 325/326, `_b` = 337/338 and 339/340 | rod plots |
| `06_ctr_rod_comparisons/rod_pairs.csv` | rms of ln(ratio) for rods compared in measurement order (repeats, back-to-back pairs) | `fig_robust` panel b |
| `07_ctr_oh_fraction/` | OH fraction per film and potential from the CTR fits under the bridging-O assumptions (Rao; 0 Å; fitted), best and range | `fig_variants` |
| `08_ctr_to_cv/` | Change predicted from the CTR-fitted states at each CTR potential, and measured vs predicted at 0.15, 0.35, 0.7 V | `fig_ctr2cv` |
| `09_model_sensitivity/` | Model change at each CV reflection for an OH change (0.3 to 0.7) and a film-spacing change (+0.1%) | sensitivity / control arguments |
| `10_work_function/` | KPFM work function vs thickness (Jeong et al. 2026, different samples), read from their plot | backup B5 |
| `11_cv_synced/` | Clock-synced CV results: change from 0 to 0.7 V per scan (`response.csv`), per reflection with the 4.1 nm ratios (`summary.csv`), half-way potentials (`halfway.csv`); made by `../make_plot_data_sync.py` | slide 6b, slides 7 and 12b numbers |
| `12_ec/` | EC checks: day-4 holds and the current fits of every CV (`day4_holds.csv`, `cv_current_fits.csv`); the current during SPEC scans and gaps of three long holds, with each scan's transmission (`beam_*`) | backups B8, B9 |

Timing of the stationary scans:
- **Period from the data:** scans with a clear response.
- **Nominal 0.3 images/s:** flat scans and controls. Their E axis is indicative only. Their amplitudes in
  `03_cv_summary` do not depend on timing.
- **147:** period from its Ru peaks (72 images), hold at the logged Start (image 50), no shift. It is hysteretic.
- **343:** period taken from 341.
